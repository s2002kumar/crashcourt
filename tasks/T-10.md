## What the checker does

1. **Engine and result model.** `check_trial(artifacts, registry, registry_sha256)` returns schema v1's
   `ResultRecord` and `checker.json`. Output bytes are canonical. A frozen checker first verifies itself and
   the trial's landmark table against `FROZEN.md` and refuses to classify on any difference.
2. **Predicates separate from classification.** Layer A returns ANOMALY, NO_ANOMALY, or NOT_EXERCISED with
   evidence pointers and named witnesses; Layer B reads only those.
3. **Unexercised invariants are omitted**, listed in `checker.json` with the missing witness. With nothing
   exercised there is no `result.json`.
4. **Fault not fired is INVALID.** Every arm has exactly one `fp.hit` with the declared action (trigger 10);
   every hit sits at its declared landmark, at the landmark's audited occurrence (11); every declared fault
   shows its 3.6 symptoms (12).
5. **I3 is a harness alarm**: INVALID trigger 15, never an outcome (decision 4).
6. **Clause polarity from the registry, grades from the mapping.**
7. **Describes clauses never carry PASS** (decision 1), checked by the property test.
8. **EXPECTED only with every condition witnessed.**
9. **VIOLATION_CANDIDATE requirements**: a carrying guarantee in scope that forbids the anomaly, no permission
   witnessed, verified faults, and, for the invariants about what a second run does, an undisclaimed thread
   with no harness-originated event. The checker never writes VIOLATION.
10. **Synthetic tests** for every invariant on W1 (approve, reject, misuse), W5, a forced fork, kills before
    and after a node's end, LG-PW-EXC's exception case, a loss before the first checkpoint, network and
    database faults, a double resume, a client's retry, and the held-barrier K3 and N8 shapes.
11. **Mutation tests.** AM1-AM10 give the named outcomes; each predicate has a mutant that makes it fail.
12. **Freeze.** `FROZEN.md` holds the sha256 of every checker file, the registry, each effect model, and each
    landmark table. `verify_checker` (the batch gate), `verify_trial` (per trial), and `verify_scenario` (a
    cell's `landmarks: <name>` key against the frozen tables) refuse any difference.

## My rules and rulings of 2026-10-10

- **client.fail is observational.** It never says the run changed nothing; the oracle decides.
- **What a controlled fault leaves is evaluated, not invalidated.** Rows a killed worker's in-flight saver
  call commits between the two snapshots are evaluated on the second, and noted; any other difference is
  INVALID (trigger 7).
- **Trigger 16, "uncontrolled trial"**: the orchestrator marked the trial invalid; a worker ended with a
  signal or status no declared fault explains; an orchestrator fault struck a worker no declared barrier held.
- **Triggers 1 and 3**: mismatched provenance; missing causal evidence.
- **Running at a stop**: `node.start` occurred and its matching `node.end` had not occurred before the stop.
  A node that had ended with its task's writes in flight was not running; pending-write semantics own that
  case. So K1a, A1, and N3 read GAP on the duplicate, with the reason naming pending-write semantics.
- **L-RES** is the `put_writes` carrying the RESUME write for the interrupted task, not the null task's copy.
- **Landmark tables**: a fault scenario names its table with `landmarks: <name>`; a fault-free one needs none.
- **D1**: LGP-DT's disclaimer and harness-originated events apply only to I2 and I4-I8. I1a, I1b, and I11 are
  always judged: they state no writer condition, and no second run can produce their anomalies (I1a is one
  process's own order; no graph run deletes saver rows).
- **D2**: I4 passes under LG-PW-EXC only where a node actually raised; otherwise no anomaly is
  NO_ANOMALY_UNSPECIFIED, with the reason.
- **D3**: one outcome per invariant, the most severe, in the order VIOLATION_CANDIDATE, GAP, EXPECTED, PASS,
  NO_ANOMALY_UNSPECIFIED (INVALID is the trial's validity, not part of the order). `checker.json`'s
  `details` keeps every anomaly with its own outcome, clause, case, evidence, and every witness.
- **E1** leaves the first matrix before the freeze (below). No landmark or permission is invented for it.
- **K3 and N8** are re-specified with deterministic held barriers, not SIGSTOP: K3 replicates the
  concurrent-writer outcome, N8 is held stale worker, takeover, stale-worker release. SIGSTOP robustness can
  come after the first matrix.
- **Instrument controls match observations, not verdicts**: K1a's success criterion is the published
  observable shape (two charge rows, attempts 1 and 2), which `checker.json`'s detail carries whatever the
  classification; K4 / C11-8764 is an observation-only instrument check ("accepted run with no durable
  record") with no outcome fabricated.
- **K1b, N4, A2, and A3's I2** take NO_ANOMALY_UNSPECIFIED or GAP under LG-PW-CRASH.

## E1, deferred before the freeze

E1 was K1a under `exit` durability, predicting I4 EXPECTED under LG-DUR-EXIT plus LG-INT-RERUN. Under exit
durability LangGraph makes no `put_writes` call during a run and saves checkpoints and writes only when the
loop exits (langgraph 1.2.12, `pregel/_loop.py:466`, `:1134`, `:1324-1334`), so this workload never reaches
L-EXW, and the cell's deterministic trigger cannot be derived from a real fault-free history (T-09 recorded
none under exit). Nothing in the checker maps LG-DUR-EXIT for I4. This is a pre-freeze change to the
pre-registration; no evidence has run.

## The landmark audit

Derived from T-09's final corpus (batches `t09-*-20261010T093643Z`, in research/evidence/t-09/) and identical
in the development corpus (194c94a): each landmark matched once per run, at the same occurrence in all 50
trials of its batch. Occurrences count calls of the method in one worker and op, as T-06's registry does.

| Landmark | Rule | Where | Occurrence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L-IN | `put`, source input, no `node.start` before it in the op | W1 start-1; every W5 turn | 1 |
| L-RES | `put_writes` with `__resume__`, of the approval node's task | W1 resume-1 | 2 |
| L-EXW | `put_writes` of execute's task, after execute's `node.end` | W1 resume-1 | 3 |

The table is audited and records each occurrence; an arm naming a landmark must name it (trigger 11).

## The post-trial evidence contract

Stated in `crashcourt/checker/load.py` and frozen with it; the runner writes it under #26.

- `snapshot.json`: `crashcourt.snapshot.dump.take_pair` on the direct oracle DSN after quiescence (every
  started worker exited and reaped, every fault healed), for every scenario thread, with the pre-trial count
  from `pretrial_rows` before the first dispatch. Two snapshots at least two seconds apart by the database
  clock (`SnapshotMeta.taken_ns`), holding the same rows; no toxic installed.
- `ledger.json`: `LedgerExport.from_sink(GET /ledger rows, GET /ledger/verify)` after quiescence, for every
  trial.
- `sink-health.json`: `[SinkHealth.from_reading(before, "before"), SinkHealth.from_reading(after, "after")]`,
  "before" after the sink opened the trial and before the first dispatch.
- Evidence only: nothing read for them decides the schedule, a fault, or a retry. Missing or malformed
  evidence fails closed: no file rather than a partial one, and an absent or unparsable file is trigger 1.

## Outside the freeze, for my decision

- The mapping from a T-07 scenario, run.json, and the manifest to the checker's `TrialScenario` is not in the
  frozen checker: #26 builds it on the runner's side (one builder, which T-13's verification also calls).
  T-13's commit pinning covers it today, not `FROZEN.md`.
- Arms carry no landmark id in T-07's scenario format; the builder infers it from the method and the audited
  occurrence, which can only fail closed (trigger 11 re-verifies both). An explicit per-step `landmark` would
  be a scenario-schema change.
- K3 and N8 need a takeover (the op dispatched to a second worker while the first is held), which T-07's
  scenarios cannot yet express.

## Review

A review against the specification found two blockers and four major problems, all fixed with regression
tests (`test_checker_regressions.py`): a network fault that never touched its call verified (now R20); I1a
paired a lost-ack put with a later run (pairs are within one run); trigger 6 was too loose, any non-zero exit
was a stop, and undeclared faults fed the stop witnesses (R21-R23); I7a gave EXPECTED on a disclaimed thread
(R24); a frozen trial without the frozen landmark table raised (R25). Before the freeze, the held N8 shape
showed I1a pairing a stale worker's start with a takeover's checkpoint through a shared task id; I1a now
pairs each start with the checkpoint it records (R8).
